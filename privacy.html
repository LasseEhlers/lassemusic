Privacy policy — Lasse Uploads

Lasse Uploads is a private tool used solely by Lasse Ehlers to upload videos to his own YouTube channels (Lasse Music, LasseCash Crypto World News).

  • It accesses only the YouTube account of the person who authorizes it, via Google's official YouTube Data API.
  • It uploads videos, sets titles, descriptions and thumbnails on those channels. Nothing else.
  • It does not collect, store, share or sell any personal data of any third party. OAuth tokens are stored locally on the owner's own computer and never transmitted elsewhere.
  • No cookies, no analytics, no tracking.
